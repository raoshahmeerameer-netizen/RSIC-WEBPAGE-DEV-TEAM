03 Why RSIC?

Come for the challenge. Stay for the chaos.

Five things happen at RSIC, more or less in this order. Scroll.

  1. 01

    Challenge

    Problems designed to make you stop, think, rethink. Then move. Nothing here is solved on the first read.

  2. 02

    Collaboration

    Different disciplines. Different people. A physicist's instinct and a sociologist's questions, aimed at the same problem.

  3. 03

    Competition

    Pressure turns good ideas into unforgettable ones. Judges who know the discipline, and know when you're bluffing.

  4. 04

    Chaos

    The unexpected is part of the experiment. Plans change mid-round. Adapting is part of the score.

  5. 05

    Moments

    The kind you remember long after the scoreboard disappears. The room, the argument, the win nobody expected.
