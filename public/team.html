A message from the team

We survived the planning.

An idea, a vision, and a whole lot of hard work later, RSIC is finally here!

A year in the making, countless meetings, 2 AM work sessions, a few collective crash-outs, and more than a handful of strikes later, what once seemed like an idea has finally become a reality.

Somewhere along the way, though, the planning became its own little reality show. From Sabika’s irreverent daily quotes to Khadija’s constant rants, from Ilma’s crash-outs to Alizah and Zayna’s tea-dropping sessions, from Meerab’s occasional disappearances to Laveazay making sure to call her out every chance she got, and Hussain surviving as the only man in the E.C. The Executive Council has had its fair share of plot twists. At this point, organising RSIC has been just as much about keeping up with the EC as it has been about organising the event itself.

But between the chaos, the laughter, the last-minute changes, and the endless meetings, one thing has remained constant: our excitement to bring this vision to life. What started as an idea has grown into a platform for young minds to experiment, collaborate, challenge ideas, and think beyond the ordinary.

We are incredibly excited to welcome you to the Ravi Science and Innovation Competition (RSIC), the first event of its kind at The City School Ravi Campus. Three days of science, innovation, curiosity, and a little healthy competition await, and we cannot wait to see what you all bring to the table.

To all our delegates, thank you for being a part of this milestone. We hope you bring your curiosity, your competitive spirit, and perhaps a little patience for the unexpected. After all, if the Executive Council has taught us anything, it is that things rarely go exactly as planned, but they do make for a good story.

The Executive Council may have been a whole event in itself, but now it is your turn to take centre stage.

Welcome to RSIC 2026. We survived the planning; now it’s your turn to make it worth it!

With excitement (and a little relief),

The RSIC Executive Council
