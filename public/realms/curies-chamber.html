Realms/04 Chemistry

Curie's Chamber

Reactions don't negotiate.

Curie's Chamber pairs theoretical chemistry with a supervised practical round, where the real test isn't knowing the reaction. It's running it safely, correctly, and on time.

04
Theme

Theory meets bench work

Format

Written round + supervised practical

Team size

1 to 2

Duration

3 hours

Rules

  1. Safety briefing is mandatory before the practical round.
  2. Any breach of lab safety protocol results in disqualification from that round.
  3. Written round is closed-book.
  4. Practical apparatus is provided. No personal equipment.
  5. Results must be logged in the provided lab notebook.

Judging criteria

  • Written accuracy30%
  • Practical technique30%
  • Result accuracy25%
  • Safety conduct15%

Requirements

  • Safety goggles (provided)
  • Closed-toe shoes, hair tied back
  • Delegate ID
  • No loose sleeves or scarves in the practical round

Schedule & materials

  • Full timings are released with the event schedule on the Updates page.
  • Room and stage assignments are confirmed the week of RSIC.

Heads upStudents who miss the safety briefing cannot enter the practical round under any circumstances.
