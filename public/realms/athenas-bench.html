Realms/10 Law & Sociology

Athena's Bench

Make sense of the mess, and look like a genius doing it.

This is the category where we take super-serious legal codes and hurl them into the chaotic dumpster fire of human behaviour. If you turned up expecting to memorise fancy Latin, wear a sharp blazer and yell "Objection!" like a TV lawyer, lower your expectations immediately. You aren't reciting what the law says. You're here to explain why a rule written by a man in a powdered wig two centuries ago collapses the second real, weird human beings try to follow it.
