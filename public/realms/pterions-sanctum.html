Realms/07 Psychology

Pterion's Sanctum

The mind, under observation.

Pterion's Sanctum runs case-based psychology. Reading a scenario, building a defensible diagnosis or intervention, and holding that position against a panel trained to find the gap in your reasoning.

07
Theme

Applied clinical + social reasoning

Format

Case study + panel defence

Team size

1 to 2

Duration

3 hours

Rules

  1. Case materials are released at the start of the round, not before.
  2. No prior-published case studies may be referenced verbatim.
  3. Panel questioning lasts up to 12 minutes per team.
  4. Ethical framing is scored, not optional.
  5. Notes may be handwritten only. No digital references.

Judging criteria

  • Diagnostic reasoning35%
  • Ethical framing25%
  • Defence25%
  • Clarity15%

Requirements

  • Delegate ID
  • Notebook and pen
  • Printed case materials (provided on arrival)
  • Formal attire recommended, not required

Schedule & materials

  • Full timings are released with the event schedule on the Updates page.
  • Room and stage assignments are confirmed the week of RSIC.

Heads upPanel questions can challenge your position directly. Changing your stance under good reasoning scores higher than defending a flawed one.
