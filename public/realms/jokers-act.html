Realms/09 Film & Drama

The Joker's Act

Make it believable.

The Joker's Act is a live performance round. Scripted or devised, judged on craft rather than spectacle. What's rewarded is control: pacing, presence, and a piece that knows exactly what it's trying to do.

09
Theme

Devised or scripted performance

Format

Team performance

Team size

2 to 6

Duration

10-minute slot

Rules

  1. Performance time limit is strict. Overtime is penalised per minute.
  2. Sets and props must be self-carried and self-struck within 3 minutes.
  3. Content must be submitted for review before the event.
  4. No recorded backing vocals for live musical elements.
  5. Technical cues (lighting/sound) must be submitted 48 hours in advance.

Judging criteria

  • Performance craft35%
  • Direction & pacing25%
  • Concept25%
  • Technical execution15%

Requirements

  • Reviewed script or performance outline
  • Own props/costumes
  • Cue sheet for tech
  • Delegate ID for all performers

Schedule & materials

  • Full timings are released with the event schedule on the Updates page.
  • Room and stage assignments are confirmed the week of RSIC.

Heads upContent flagged during review must be revised and resubmitted before the performance is confirmed on the schedule.
