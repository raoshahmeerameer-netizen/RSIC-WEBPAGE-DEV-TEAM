Realms/08 Environmental Business

Gaia's Exchange

Sustainability, priced correctly.

Gaia's Exchange is a pitch-and-negotiate round where environmental responsibility has to survive contact with a balance sheet. Teams build a venture, then defend its numbers to judges playing investors.

08
Theme

Sustainable venture strategy

Format

Team pitch + negotiation

Team size

2 to 4

Duration

Half day

Rules

  1. Financial model must be submitted before the live pitch.
  2. Pitch is capped at 8 minutes, strictly timed.
  3. Negotiation round follows immediately. No preparation break.
  4. All figures must be justified on request.
  5. One judge acts as a hostile investor by design. Expect pushback.

Judging criteria

  • Viability30%
  • Environmental impact25%
  • Financial model25%
  • Pitch & negotiation20%

Requirements

  • Financial model (spreadsheet, submitted in advance)
  • Pitch deck (max 10 slides)
  • Delegate ID
  • Printed one-page summary for judges

Schedule & materials

  • Full timings are released with the event schedule on the Updates page.
  • Room and stage assignments are confirmed the week of RSIC.

Heads upNumbers that don't hold up under questioning are penalised more heavily than modest, defensible ones.
