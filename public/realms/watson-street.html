Realms/04 Biology

Case 1: The Watson Street

Think sharp. Investigate deeper. Solve the case.

Think biology is all cells, diagrams and words that make no sense? Not on Watson Street. Here you investigate a murder case yourself, where every clue matters and nothing is quite what it seems. From CCTV footage and crime-scene evidence to hidden secrets, you'll think outside the box, connect the clues and trust your instincts. Just when you think you've figured it out, a new plot twist. The case keeps changing, the clues keep coming, and your next move could change everything.
