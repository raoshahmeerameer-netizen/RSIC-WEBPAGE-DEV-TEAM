Realms/01 Mathematics

The Cartesian Realm

Prove it, or it didn't happen.

The Cartesian Realm treats mathematics as a live event, not a worksheet. Expect proof-writing under time pressure, problem sets that reward elegant solutions over brute force, and a judging panel that reads your work like a referee report.

01
Theme

Precision under time pressure

Format

Individual + team rounds

Team size

1 to 3

Duration

3 hours, two rounds

Rules

  1. Round 1 is individual; Round 2 is team-based.
  2. Calculators permitted only where stated on the problem sheet.
  3. Work must show full reasoning. Final answers alone score zero.
  4. No communication between individual-round competitors.
  5. Disputes go to the head judge before results are finalised.

Judging criteria

  • Correctness40%
  • Elegance of proof25%
  • Time efficiency20%
  • Presentation15%

Requirements

  • Valid delegate ID
  • Own stationery (no shared calculators)
  • Scientific calculator if specified
  • Arrive 15 minutes before Round 1

Schedule & materials

  • Full timings are released with the event schedule on the Updates page.
  • Room and stage assignments are confirmed the week of RSIC.

Heads upLate arrivals after Round 1 begins forfeit that round's score but may still compete in Round 2.
