Realms/11 Robotics

Theseus' Circuit

Build it. Test it. Take it to the circuit.

Think you can build a machine that can actually think its way out of trouble? Welcome to Theseus' Circuit, a three-round robotics challenge that takes teams from building a robot from the ground up to testing its performance and competing in an autonomous Robo Soccer finale.

Across three days, teams will build, tune, and compete, putting their engineering, programming, and problem-solving skills to the test. From electronics and mechanical design to sensors and autonomous control, every stage challenges teams to turn an idea into a machine that can perform when it matters.

The three rounds

  1. Build. Teams put a robot together from the ground up, covering electronics and mechanical design.
  2. Test. The robot is run and tuned, and its performance is measured.
  3. Robo Soccer. The finale is autonomous, so the robot plays without a hand on the controls.

What it asks of you

  • Engineering and mechanical design
  • Electronics
  • Programming and autonomous control
  • Sensors
  • Problem solving when the machine misbehaves
