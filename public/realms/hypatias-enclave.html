Hypatia's Enclave

Fluency in the architecture of thought.

Deep within the ruins of a collapsed intelligence lies Hypatia's Enclave, a domain where circuitry and cognition are inseparable. To pass through it, delegates must prove fluency not only in code but in the architecture of thought itself: theory, logic, computation, and the discipline of tracing a mind that has gone astray.
