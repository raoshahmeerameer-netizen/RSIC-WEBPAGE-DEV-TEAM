Questions

Asked and answered.

If yours isn't here, the numbers below reach a person who can answer it.

What do I bring?

Your delegate ID, plus whatever your realm lists under Requirements on its page.

How long is it?

Three days, from Friday 13 to Sunday 15 November. Rounds run from a 10-minute slot in The Joker's Act to a full day in Project Nexus or Athena's Bench.

Do I need a team?

Yes. RSIC is entered by delegations, not by individuals. Your school sends 5 to 7 delegates who cover the realms you pick between them.

How many realms do we enter?

Four to six. One core realm, either Project Nexus or Gaia's Exchange, and then 3 to 5 more from the other nine realms.
