What do I bring?

Your delegate ID, plus whatever your realm lists under Requirements on its page.

How long is it?

Three days, from Friday 13 to Sunday 15 November, starting at 1:45 PM PKT. Rounds run from a 10-minute slot in The Joker's Act to a full day in Project Nexus or Athena's Bench.

Do I need a team?

Yes. RSIC is entered by delegations, not by individuals. Your school sends 5 to 7 delegates who cover the realms you pick between them.

How many realms do we enter?

Between 5 and 8. Project Nexus and Gaia's Exchange are compulsory, and you choose the rest from the other nine realms.
